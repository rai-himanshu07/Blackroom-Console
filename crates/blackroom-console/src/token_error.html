The address must carry the secret token the console printed when it started. This page does not say whether the token was missing, old or mistyped.

How to get a working address

  1. On the laptop, read the address again: cat "$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/blackroom-console/url", or look at the terminal where the console was started. Open that address on this device.
  2. If you do not have access to the laptop, ask its owner for the address.
  3. Check the address bar before you type anything: do not click through a certificate warning you did not expect.
