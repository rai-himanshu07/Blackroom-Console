Blackroom Console
Disconnect was not confirmed. The laptop may still be controlled, and a Private session may still hold its screen and keyboard, until it notices this device is gone.
  1. Wait about 30 seconds: the laptop ends a session by itself when this device goes silent.
  2. On the laptop, hold Left Ctrl + Left Shift + Left Alt + Esc for 2 seconds. This works only while the keyboard is blocked (Private with "Block this laptop's keyboard and touchpad" on). It releases the keyboard and ends every remote login until the owner reopens it at the laptop.
  3. From another device with SSH: systemctl --user stop blackroom-console.service
  4. A blank panel after that is restored by the laptop; you then see its lock screen: type the laptop password. Do not unlock it from SSH.
  5. Full steps: emergency-recovery.md in /usr/share/doc/blackroom-console/ on the laptop.
